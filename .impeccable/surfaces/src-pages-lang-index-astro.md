# Home page (public site, FR + EN)

Scope: the public home page of lescalades (Astro route `src/pages/[lang]/index.astro`, FR and EN mirrored). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: families and groups of 8 to 10 choosing a holiday house in the Gard months ahead, arriving from the Airbnb listing or a recommendation. Job: decide this is the house, then check dates. Action: "Check dates" (a request-then-approve flow later). Proof: the 40 photos, 4.88 from 32 Airbnb reviews, named hosts with a phone number, the honest facts (stairs, pool waiver, minimum stays).
Constraints: prices come from PriceLabs, never hand-kept; no invented reviews or quotes; the registration number slot must exist; French and English are equals.
Chosen by james 2026-09-20: direction C, "the modern rental site", with "little bits of Provençal charm in places" across the rest of the site. Script face: La Belle Aurore (his choice). Caps and body: Poppins.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shape every good rental site has in 2026 (full-bleed photo hero, pill actions, floating review card, list beside a photo, hosts, FAQ, dark footer), built properly in this house's own colours, with Provençal charm carried by material details rather than by layout: handwritten captions, an indienne edge pattern, a shutter-blue plane, terracotta marks, an olive sprig. It refuses the cream-and-serif "villa brochure" and the icon-tile feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: White and off-white #F7F7F4 grounds, cream #F0EDE6 section fields, olive #3F5721 for pills and links, deep green #2F4A1E for the reviews field, shutter blue #9CAFB6 once (the area section), terracotta #C79C71 as a 2px mark, near-black #1F2321 footer. Poppins 300/500/600 sentence case; La Belle Aurore only for handwritten captions and one line on the review card. Rounded corners 16px, pills 999px, soft offset shadows. Indienne repeat (green on cream, 3-lobed sprig) on section edges only, never behind text. Drawn line icons, one stroke weight.

STORY: A visitor sees the pool under the château, learns in one strip that it sleeps 10 with 4 bedrooms and a private pool, believes it through the rooms and the reviews, meets John and Laure, reads the awkward facts before they are surprised by them, and taps Check dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Photo 1 full-bleed to 100dvh; nav top (brand left, four items and FR/EN pill right); heading top-left at 4rem, 3 lines, "A village house with a pool, under the château"; one sentence beneath; white pill "Check dates" and ghost pill "See the house"; the review card bottom-right (script "Loved by guests", 4.88 with five drawn stars, 32 reviews on Airbnb). The primary action is the white pill.

FORM: The category canon, index C of the three cards (assigned A, pick B, canon C); james chose C over the roll. Seed key 1a9e7eae, mode persuade, roll degraded (no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Signature moment: the FR/EN switch swaps every line in place; the rooms gallery captions are handwritten.
Unresolved: domain, site voice (we/John and Laure), distances to verify, the 4th bedroom, host email, registration number, whether to mention the Lédenon circuit.
